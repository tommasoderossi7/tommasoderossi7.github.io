Request

Build a data-driven foundation to design marketing campaigns for a major Italian fruit producer by extracting actionable leverage points from their audience and their competitors’ audiences. The goal was to understand what people associate with the product/brand, how interest evolves over time, and which topics and stakeholders can be activated to increase reach and campaign effectiveness—then package the insights in an interactive dashboard usable by marketing teams.

My Role

I was the end-to-end owner of the analytical workstream. I aligned on objectives with the client, defined the analytical framework (what “leverage” means in measurable terms), and translated campaign questions into a set of KPIs, segmentations, and dashboard views that could directly drive creative and targeting decisions.

I designed and implemented the data pipeline (collection, normalization, enrichment), built the NLP logic for topic discovery and classification, engineered the audience and competitor comparison metrics (e.g., overlap and differentiation), and delivered the final live dashboard enabling topic exploration, trend analysis, and identification of high-impact accounts/content for activation.

Features

  • Topic clustering for content pillars: automatically groups conversations/queries into interpretable themes (e.g., product talk, territory/origin, wellness, varieties) to define campaign angles and editorial pillars.
  • Trend and seasonality tracking: time-series views of topic volume/interest to identify peaks, emerging narratives, and the best moments to launch or reinforce campaign messages.
  • Audience vs competitor comparison: overlap-style analysis to quantify how much audience is shared vs unique, supporting differentiation strategy and competitor-aware messaging.
  • Public demand and intent analysis: breaks down queries into brand vs product focus and classifies intent (informational vs commercial), clarifying whether the audience needs education, reassurance, recipes/uses, or conversion-oriented messaging.
  • Influencer / key-account surfacing: identifies and exports high-impact accounts based on engagement signals (e.g., retweets) and topic relevance for seeding, partnerships, or paid amplification.
  • Creative and keyword “suggester”: a searchable view of recurring terms and associations to accelerate creative ideation, copy lines, and keyword/SEO planning.

Methodology

  • Multi-source data ingestion: collected audience and competitor signals from social listening sources (posts, engagement metrics, authors) and complementary demand data (queries/keywords and related terms), then standardized entities, timestamps, and text fields in an ETL workspace.
  • NLP preprocessing and enrichment: cleaned and normalized text, extracted key terms/tags, and enriched records with topic labels and campaign-relevant metadata (brand vs product, sentiment-/concern-like patterns where applicable, etc.).
  • Clustering + classification layer: applied semantic clustering to build stable topic groups and trained lightweight classifiers for intent segmentation (informational vs commercial) and brand/product attribution.
  • Audience analytics: computed competitor comparisons (overlap and uniqueness), ranked accounts by topic relevance and engagement, and produced time-series metrics to detect peaks and recurring patterns.
  • Decision-ready visualization: published the full output into an interactive dashboard with filters (topic, date range, text, engagement), drilldowns to underlying posts/accounts, and exportable lists for activation and campaign planning.
